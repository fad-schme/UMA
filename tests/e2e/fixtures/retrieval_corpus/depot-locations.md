# Meridian Depot Network

The network operates four depots. Northgate is the largest, with capacity for 140 vehicles, and it is the only depot fitted with the drivetrain pit and ramp strip-down bay required for a C-service. Northgate also houses the central parts store and the depot time server that all validators synchronise against overnight.

Southcross has capacity for 95 vehicles and is the base for the night network. All six night routes begin and end at Southcross, and the night driver mess and rest facilities are located there. Southcross can perform A-services and B-services but not C-services.

Harbour Row is the smallest depot with capacity for 40 vehicles. It exists primarily to shorten dead-running on the eastern routes and has no maintenance capability beyond the daily walkaround and fluid top-ups. Vehicles based at Harbour Row are driven to Northgate or Southcross for all scheduled servicing.

Westfield has capacity for 110 vehicles and performs A-services and B-services. Westfield is the designated overflow depot during incidents: when another depot becomes unavailable, its allocated vehicles are redistributed to Westfield first because Westfield holds the largest reserve of unallocated parking.

Only Northgate and Southcross are staffed twenty-four hours. Harbour Row is staffed from 04:30 to 21:00 and Westfield from 04:00 to 23:30. Meridian Cards can be issued in person at Northgate, Southcross and Westfield, but not at Harbour Row, which has no staffed counter.
