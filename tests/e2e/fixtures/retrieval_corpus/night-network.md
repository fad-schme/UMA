# The Meridian Night Network

The night network operates between 00:30 and 05:15, seven nights a week, on six routes numbered N1 through N6. Night routes do not follow the daytime alignment of the numbered routes they resemble; the N4, for example, runs via Harbour Row rather than the Central Loop because the loop is closed for cleaning between 01:00 and 04:00.

Headways on the night network are twenty minutes on the N1, N2 and N3, and forty minutes on the N4, N5 and N6. There is no timetable padding at terminals, so a night service that is delayed inbound will depart late outbound rather than waiting to recover the schedule.

All night vehicles are low-floor and wheelchair accessible. Audio and visual next-stop announcements run on the night network exactly as they do during the day, and the driver is required to deploy the ramp on request at any designated stop.

Night services stop only at designated night stops, which are marked with a blue reflective band on the pole. Between designated stops the driver may set down a passenger on request after 01:00 if the passenger asks and the location is judged safe. This set-down-on-request rule applies to alighting only, never to boarding.

The night network carries a flat fare of 3.40 credits with no cash option; every night vehicle is card-only. The daily fare cap continues to apply across the boundary between one service day and the next, and the service day is deemed to end at 04:00.
