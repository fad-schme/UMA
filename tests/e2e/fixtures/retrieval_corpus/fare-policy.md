# Meridian Transit Fare Policy

The standard single-journey adult fare on the Meridian network is 3.40 credits when paid with a Meridian Card and 4.60 credits when paid in cash at the farebox. The cash surcharge exists to recover the cost of coin handling at the depots and is reviewed by the Fare Board every second year.

Concession fares apply to three groups. Riders aged sixty-five and over pay 1.70 credits per journey at all times. Registered students pay 2.10 credits on weekdays and travel free on weekends. Holders of a Mobility Access Pass travel free on every service, and one accompanying companion also travels free when the pass is presented to the driver.

Transfers are free between any two Meridian services within ninety minutes of the first validation. The ninety-minute window is measured from the moment of first validation, not from boarding, and it does not restart on the second leg. A third leg within the same window is also free, but a fourth leg is charged as a new journey.

The daily fare cap is 9.80 credits for adults and 4.90 credits for concession holders. Once the cap is reached, all further journeys that day are free. The cap is calculated per card, not per rider, so a card shared between two people will reach the cap faster and this is treated as fare evasion under Section 14 of the network bylaws.

Refunds for overcharges are processed through the Meridian app within five working days. Cash refunds are not issued at depots under any circumstances.
