# Service Incident Response

An incident is declared by the control room when a disruption is expected to exceed thirty minutes or to affect more than one route. Declaration is a control room decision and cannot be initiated by a driver, although any driver may request that the control room consider a declaration.

Incidents are graded one to three. A Grade 1 incident affects a single route for under two hours and is managed by the duty controller alone. A Grade 2 incident affects multiple routes or a single route for more than two hours and requires the network manager to be notified within fifteen minutes. A Grade 3 incident involves injury, a depot becoming unavailable, or any disruption expected to run past the start of the next service day, and requires the network manager on site.

During a declared incident the control room may reassign any on-duty driver without consent, may hold vehicles at terminals, and may suspend the ninety-minute transfer window so that riders are not charged again for a re-routed journey. Suspension of the transfer window is logged and reported to the Fare Board monthly.

When a depot becomes unavailable its vehicles are redistributed to Westfield first. If Westfield reaches capacity the remaining vehicles are held on-street at designated relief points, which are surveyed annually and are never within 200 metres of a residential frontage.

Incident logs are retained for seven years and are the source record for any refund dispute.
