# The Meridian Card and Ticketing Platform

The Meridian Card is a contactless stored-value card issued free of charge at any staffed depot or by post within ten working days. The card holds a maximum balance of 200 credits and cannot be topped up beyond that ceiling. Balances do not expire, but a card that has been unused for thirty-six consecutive months is deactivated and the remaining balance is transferred to the network accessibility fund.

Validators are mounted at every vehicle door and at the entrance to each enclosed station concourse. A validator flashes green for a successful validation, amber when the ninety-minute transfer window is being applied, and red when the balance is insufficient. A red flash does not lock the card; the rider may top up and validate again immediately.

The Meridian app mirrors the physical card and can be used on any handset with near-field communication. The app additionally exposes journey history for the previous eighteen months, refund requests, and a live balance. Journey history is not available at validators or from drivers, who can see only the current balance and the pass type.

Validator clocks are synchronised nightly against the depot time server. When a validator loses synchronisation by more than four seconds it enters permissive mode: it accepts every tap, records the journey as unpriced, and raises a maintenance ticket. Unpriced journeys are never billed retrospectively to the rider.

Lost cards are replaced for 5 credits with the balance carried across, provided the card was registered in the app before it was lost.
