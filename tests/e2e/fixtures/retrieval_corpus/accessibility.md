# Accessibility Standards on the Meridian Network

Every vehicle in the Meridian fleet is low-floor with a manually deployed ramp at the second door. Ramp deployment is a driver duty and may not be refused. The driver must deploy the ramp on request at any designated stop, and the boarding of a wheelchair user takes precedence over schedule recovery.

Each vehicle carries two wheelchair bays and four priority seats. The wheelchair bay is allocated on a first-come basis, but a buggy occupying the bay must be folded and moved when a wheelchair user boards. Drivers are instructed to make the request once over the public address system and to escalate to the control room rather than to argue with a passenger who refuses.

Audio and visual next-stop announcements are fitted to the whole fleet and cannot be disabled by the driver. When the announcement system fails, the driver must call the stops manually and report the fault at the end of the shift.

The Mobility Access Pass is issued on the basis of a clinical assessment and is valid for three years. It permits free travel on every service at every hour, including the night network, and it extends free travel to one accompanying companion. Applications are assessed by the accessibility office within twenty working days.

Assistance animals travel free and are permitted anywhere on the vehicle including the wheelchair bay. Drivers may not ask for documentation of an assistance animal's status.
