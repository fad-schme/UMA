# Driver Rostering Rules

A Meridian driver may not be rostered for more than nine hours of driving in any twenty-four hour period, and no single continuous driving stretch may exceed four hours and thirty minutes without a break of at least thirty minutes. Breaks may be split into two periods of at least fifteen minutes each, but only on day duties.

Night duties are governed by a stricter rule. A night duty is any duty with more than two hours of driving between 00:30 and 05:15. Night duties are capped at seven hours of driving, breaks may not be split, and a driver may not work more than four consecutive night duties before taking two full calendar days off. Night duties are rostered exclusively from Southcross.

The minimum rest period between the end of one duty and the start of the next is eleven hours. This may be reduced to nine hours no more than twice in any seven-day period, and each reduction must be compensated by an equivalent extension of a later rest period within the same fortnight.

Every duty roster is published fourteen days in advance. Changes inside the fourteen-day window require the driver's consent except during a declared incident, when the control room may reassign any on-duty driver without consent for the duration of the incident.

Each depot maintains an on-call list of two drivers per shift. On-call drivers must be reachable and able to report to their depot within forty-five minutes.
