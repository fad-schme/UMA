# Fleet Maintenance Schedule

Every Meridian vehicle undergoes a daily walkaround inspection before it leaves the depot. The walkaround is performed by the outgoing driver, not by a mechanic, and covers tyres, lights, ramp operation, mirrors and the fire suppression indicator. A failed ramp check removes the vehicle from service immediately; no other single walkaround failure does.

Scheduled servicing runs on a distance basis rather than a calendar basis. An A-service is performed every 8,000 kilometres and takes roughly four hours. A B-service is performed every 24,000 kilometres and takes a full working day. A C-service, which includes the drivetrain and the ramp mechanism strip-down, is performed every 96,000 kilometres and takes three days at Northgate, the only depot equipped for it.

Vehicles are rotated between depots so that no single depot accumulates a disproportionate share of high-mileage units. Rotation happens quarterly and is planned against odometer readings taken on the last day of each quarter.

The night network draws its vehicles from the same pool as the day network, which means night-allocated units still meet their service intervals on distance. Because night routes are longer per hour than day routes, night-heavy units reach the B-service threshold roughly five weeks earlier than day-only units, and the maintenance planner allows for this when building the rotation.

Any vehicle that has failed a ramp check twice within thirty days is withdrawn for a full ramp mechanism inspection regardless of its position in the service cycle.
